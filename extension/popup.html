Exam Proctor

During the exam's start and end time only, this extension reports the website open in your active tab (address without search terms, and page title), other open tabs that are not allowed, and when you switch to another app or stop using the keyboard and mouse.

Your instructor sets which sites are allowed. Visiting any other site, or leaving the browser, is flagged for review. The extension does not record keystrokes, page content, your screen, camera or microphone.
