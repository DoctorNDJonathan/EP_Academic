Exam Proctor: Student Guide

For online tests monitored with the Exam Proctor Chrome extension

Some of your online tests use Exam Proctor, a Chrome extension. For each test, your instructor chooses which websites you may use. If you open any other site, or switch to another app, you get a warning and your instructor is told. Install it once (5 minutes); after that, joining a test takes 30 seconds.

Your instructor gives youThe test code and the list of allowed sites (in advance), and the PIN (at the start of the test)
You needA laptop with Google Chrome, internet, and the extension installed beforehand
Roll numberType it exactly as it appears in the class list

What it records, only during the test

  • The address of the page in your active tab (without search terms) and its title
  • Other open tabs on sites that are not allowed
  • When you switch to another app, or stop using the keyboard and mouse

What it never records

  • Keystrokes, passwords or anything you type
  • Page content, your screen, camera or microphone
  • Anything before the test starts or after it ends

Records are deleted 90 days after each test. A person reviews every flag; nothing is decided automatically. Privacy policy: exam-proctor-rhbo.onrender.com/privacy. If you do not wish to be monitored, or cannot use Chrome, tell your instructor before the test.

1. Install it once (5 minutes)

  1. On the laptop you will use for tests, open Google Chrome. Safari, Edge and Firefox will not work.
  2. Open this link in Chrome:
    https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/jheooojginplgillkdegkhlgblcmoimd
  3. Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.
  4. Pin it: click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Exam Proctor. A small navy stopwatch icon appears in the toolbar.
  5. Click the stopwatch. You should see: a notice and a form asking for a test code, roll number and PIN.

On a Mac: allow Chrome notifications

Open System Settings → Notifications → Google Chrome and switch on Allow notifications, so the warnings can pop up.

Laptop from another organisation?

Some managed laptops block extensions. If Add to Chrome is greyed out, tell your instructor well before the test.

2. Before each test

  • Charge your laptop and check the Wi-Fi.
  • Sign in beforehand to any account the test needs, such as Google, Microsoft or Kaggle, so you don't have to sign in during the test.
  • Close every tab you don't need. Tabs on sites that are not allowed are reported even in the background.

3. Joining the test

  1. Click the stopwatch icon.
  2. Type the test code, your roll number and the PIN your instructor announces.
  3. Read the notice, tick I have read the notice…, and click Join exam.

4. During the test

Icon labelMeaningWhat to do
ONMonitoring is running and you are on an allowed siteCarry on
!You are on a site that is not allowed, or outside ChromeGo back to an allowed site straight away
No labelMonitoring is not running (before the start or after the end)Nothing
  • Stay on the allowed sites listed in the popup. Everything else, including search engines and AI tools, is reported.
  • Stay in Chrome. Switching to another app (WhatsApp, Word, another browser) is reported, unless your instructor says the test needs that app.
  • A visit of under 3 seconds, such as a mis-click you close at once, is not reported.
  • Need a site that is not on the list? Tell the invigilator. They can add it for everyone within seconds.
  • Wi-Fi drops? Carry on working. The extension saves everything and sends it when you reconnect, and the record shows it was a network problem.

5. At the end

You should see: "Exam finished. Monitoring stopped." The icon label disappears and nothing more is recorded.

Keep the extension for your next test. Never remove or disable it during a test: that is reported to your instructor. If you want to remove it, wait until the test has ended, then right-click the icon and choose Remove from Chrome.

6. If something goes wrong

You seeWhat to do
"Cannot reach the exam server"Check your internet. The server may be waking up: wait one minute and try again.
"No exam with this code"Check the code your instructor gave you.
"Incorrect exam PIN"Re-type the PIN carefully; letters must match in capitals or small letters.
"Not on the list for this exam"Check your roll number. If it is right, tell your instructor.
The ! label on a page you think is allowedCompare the address bar with the allowed list in the popup, then tell the invigilator.
Chrome crashed or the laptop restartedReopen Chrome, click the icon and join again with the same details. Nothing is lost.
Anything elseTell your instructor or invigilator straight away, with a screenshot if you can.
