# Online exams: setting up Exam Proctor

Some online exams use a Chrome extension called **Exam Proctor**. For each exam,
your instructor chooses which websites you may use. If you open any other site or
app during the exam, it is flagged for the invigilators to review.

## What it checks (only during an exam you join)

- The website in your active tab (address without search terms) and its page title
- Other open tabs on sites that are not allowed
- When you switch away from Chrome to another app, or stop using the keyboard and mouse

## What it never records

Keystrokes, page content, search terms, passwords, your screen, camera,
microphone, location, or anything before the exam starts or after it ends.

A person reviews every flag, and no decision is made automatically.
Privacy policy: https://exam-proctor-rhbo.onrender.com/privacy

## Before the practice session

1. Use **Google Chrome** on the laptop you will use for the exam.
2. Install the extension: [CHROME WEB STORE LINK]
3. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to
   **Exam Proctor**, so it stays visible.
4. Join the practice session on [DATE, TIME] using code **[PRACTICE CODE]**.
   The PIN is announced at the start.

## On exam day

1. Open Chrome **10 minutes early**. Close tabs you won't need; tabs on sites
   that aren't allowed are flagged even in the background.
2. When the PIN is announced, click the extension icon. Enter the exam code,
   your roll number and the PIN, tick the consent box, and click **Join exam**.
3. Check that the popup says **"Monitoring until …"** and shows the list of
   **allowed sites**. The icon shows a green **ON** badge.
4. Stay on the allowed sites and in Chrome until the exam ends. Monitoring stops by itself.

**If you see a red "!" badge or a warning notification,** you're on a site that isn't
allowed, or outside Chrome. Go back to the exam straight away.

## If something goes wrong

- **Your internet drops:** carry on. Activity is saved and sent when you reconnect.
- **Chrome crashes or closes:** reopen it and join again with the same details.
- **A site you need is blocked or flagged** (e.g. a login page): tell the
  invigilator at once. They can add it during the exam.
- **You get an error message:** take a screenshot and email your instructor, or nikhildj@xime.org, straight away.

Don't remove or disable the extension during an exam. The invigilators are
notified if you do.

If you do not want to be monitored, contact your instructor **before** the exam
to discuss alternative arrangements.
