Exam Proctor: Participant Guide

Faculty pilot · Monday 5 October 2026 · 11:30 to 12:30 · Old IQAC

Thank you for taking part. For one hour you will sit a short mock exam as a "student", while a Chrome extension called Exam Proctor checks that you stay on the websites the exam allows. We are testing the tool, not you: nothing is graded.

WhenMonday 5 October 2026. Please arrive by 11:15. Monitoring runs 11:30 to 12:20, then feedback until 12:30.

What it records, only from 11:30 to 12:20

  • The address of the page in your active tab (without search terms) and its title
  • Other open tabs on sites that are not allowed
  • When you switch to another app, or stop using the keyboard and mouse

What it never records

  • Keystrokes, passwords or anything you type
  • Page content, your screen, camera or microphone
  • Anything before 11:30 or after 12:20

Pilot records are deleted within 30 days. Full privacy policy: exam-proctor-rhbo.onrender.com/privacy

1. Before Monday: install the extension (5 minutes)

  1. On the laptop you will bring, open Google Chrome. Other browsers (Safari, Edge, Firefox) will not work.
  2. Open this link in Chrome:
    https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/jheooojginplgillkdegkhlgblcmoimd
    It is also in the invitation email; clicking it there is easiest.
  3. Click the blue Add to Chrome button, then Add extension in the box that appears.
  4. Pin the icon so it is always visible: click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Exam Proctor. A small navy stopwatch icon appears in the toolbar.
  5. Click the stopwatch icon. You should see: a notice and a form asking for the exam code, roll number and PIN. Close it for now; you join on Monday.
  6. Sign in to Google in Chrome and open colab.research.google.com once, to check that Colab opens. Signing in now means you will not need to sign in during the test.

On a Mac: let Chrome show notifications

Warnings appear as notifications. Open System Settings → Notifications → Google Chrome and switch on Allow notifications. If you skip this, the warnings will still show on the icon, but not as pop-ups.

Laptop managed by IT?

Some managed laptops block extensions. If Add to Chrome is greyed out or refused, reply to the invitation email before Monday so we can help.

3. Joining the exam (11:20 to 11:30)

  1. Click the Exam Proctor stopwatch icon in Chrome's toolbar.
  2. Type the exam code FACPILOT1.
  3. Type your roll number: your XIME email name, the part before @.
  4. Type the PIN shown on the screen.
  5. Read the notice, tick I have read the notice…, and click Join exam.

4. Reading the icon and the warnings

The small label on the stopwatch icon shows how you are doing at a glance:

Icon labelMeaningWhat to do
ONMonitoring is running and you are on an allowed siteNothing. Carry on
!You are on a site that is not allowed, or outside ChromeGo back to an allowed site straight away
No labelMonitoring is not running (before 11:30 or after 12:20)Nothing

When you open a site that is not allowed, a notification also appears: "This site is not allowed in this exam". If you switch to another app, it says "You left the exam browser". A visit shorter than 3 seconds, such as a mis-click you close at once, is not reported.

5. Part A: system check (11:30 to 11:37)

These four steps deliberately trigger each kind of warning, so we can check that the tool notices them. Follow the times; the organiser will also announce each step.

  1. 11:30. An allowed site. Open pandas.pydata.org and click around for a minute. You should see: the ON label.
  2. 11:32. A forbidden site. Open bbc.com/news in a new tab, wait about 10 seconds, then close that tab. You should see: a "not allowed" warning and the ! label, then ON again.
  3. 11:33. Another app. Switch to Word, Finder or File Explorer for about 10 seconds, then come back to Chrome. You should see: "You left the exam browser".
  4. 11:34. A background tab. Open youtube.com in a new tab, then click back to the pandas tab and leave YouTube open. At 11:37, close the YouTube tab.

6. Part B: the mock exam (11:38 to 12:15)

You will receive a printed question paper with 10 short questions on exploratory data analysis of the diamonds dataset, which is built into the seaborn library. Work in Google Colab.

  1. Open colab.research.google.com and choose New notebook (or File → New notebook in Drive).
  2. Type the first code cell from the question paper to load the data, and run it with Shift + Enter.
  3. Answer each question in a new code cell. Add a text cell where a question asks you to explain.

Allowed during the exam

  • colab.research.google.com: any notebook
  • accounts.google.com: Google sign-in
  • Documentation: pandas.pydata.org, seaborn.pydata.org, matplotlib.org

Everything else is reported, for example

  • Google Search, ChatGPT, Gemini or any other AI tool, Stack Overflow, YouTube
  • Email, WhatsApp or other apps on the laptop. Use your phone only for this guide
  • colab.google, which now leads to Google's developer site. Type colab.research.google.com in full

Need a site that is not on the list?

Raise your hand. The invigilator can add a site for everyone in a few seconds, and the change reaches your extension within 30 seconds.

7. At the end (12:20)

You should see: "Exam finished. Monitoring stopped." The icon label disappears and nothing more is recorded.

  1. Open the feedback form shown on the screen. It takes about 3 minutes.
  2. Keep the extension for future exams, or remove it: right-click the stopwatch icon, then Remove from Chrome. Please wait until after 12:20; removing it during the exam is reported.
